About

Who is

Born at the foot of an ancient hill ridge, Kailash rises from the Dutch lowlands with a monolithic sound forged in dirt, dust and dense forests.

Expect low-slung, psychedelic desert grooves and riffs the size of boulders, while atmospheric guitar soundscapes drift above them like an eagle crossing the canyons. We come from swamp and plain, but every song is like a pilgrimage: slow, heavy, with our gaze always pointed towards the holy mountain.
